📊 Average Apartment Rent in Korea

Average rent prices vary depending on the city and apartment type.

  • Seoul Studio Apartment: ₩700,000 ~ ₩1,500,000
  • Busan Studio Apartment: ₩400,000 ~ ₩900,000
  • Small Cities: ₩300,000 ~ ₩700,000

In popular areas such as Gangnam or Hongdae, rent may be significantly higher.

🏢 What is a Maintenance Fee in Korea?

Maintenance fees usually include:

  • Building cleaning
  • Security
  • Elevator management
  • Water bill
  • Internet

Typical maintenance fees range from ₩50,000 to ₩200,000 monthly.

🏠 Jeonse vs Monthly Rent

1. Jeonse (전세)

A large deposit with no monthly rent. The deposit is usually returned at the end of the contract.

2. Wolse (월세)

Smaller deposit with monthly rent payments. This is more common for foreigners.

⚠️ Important Tips for Foreigners

  • Always check maintenance fees before signing.
  • Utilities are often separate from rent.
  • Most rental contracts are 1–2 years.
  • Deposits are usually refundable.
  • Some landlords may require a Korean guarantor.
